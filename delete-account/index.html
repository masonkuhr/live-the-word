Delete your Live the Word account

Live the Word is made by God Creations, LLC.

Delete it in the app

  1. Open Live the Word and sign in.
  2. Go to the You tab.
  3. Tap Delete my account, then Delete forever.

Don't have the app anymore? Delete it on the web

  1. Go to livethewordapp.com.
  2. Go to the You tab and tap Sign in. Use the same Google account or email you used in the app.
  3. Tap Delete my account, then Delete forever.

Signed in with Apple on an iPhone? Delete it in the app on your iPhone.

Either way, your account is deleted right away.

Delete some of your data, and keep your account

  • A prayer request: on the Group tab, tap the trash can on your request.
  • A chat message: press and hold your message, then tap Delete message.
  • Leave a prayer group: Group tab → the group's settings → Leave group.
  • Your progress on this phone: You tab → Sign out clears it from the phone (it stays in your account).

Each of these is deleted right away.

What gets deleted

Your account and everything saved with it: your name, email, verses and progress, Walk check-ins, prayer groups you made, your prayer requests and messages, and your country setting.

What we keep

If you bought a membership, the payment record stays with Apple, Google or our payment provider (Whop) as the law requires. Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription made through Apple or Google, so cancel it there too.
