Delete your Live the Word account

Live the Word is made by God Creations, LLC.

Delete it in the app (fastest)

  1. Open Live the Word and sign in.
  2. Go to the You tab.
  3. Tap Delete account, then Delete forever.

Your account is deleted right away.

Can't open the app?

Email livethewordapp@gmail.com from the email address you signed in with, with the subject "Delete my account". We'll delete it within 30 days and write back to confirm.

What gets deleted

Your account and everything saved with it: your name, email, verses and progress, Walk check-ins, prayer groups you made, your prayer requests and messages, and your country setting.

What we keep

If you bought a membership, the payment record stays with Apple, Google or our payment provider (Whop) as the law requires. Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription made through Apple or Google, so cancel it there too.
