Privacy policy

Live the Word, by God Creations, LLC · updated November 8, 2026

We don't sell your information, we don't show ads, and we only keep what the app needs to work.

What we keep

If you sign in: your name, your email address (from Google or the email you use), the country you pick, your Bible translation, and your progress: the verses you've learned, your reviews and your streak, and whether you get our email updates. If you join a prayer group: the group's name, your daily check-in, prayer requests, chat messages and nudges. If you use the app without signing in, your progress stays on your phone or in your browser.

Who can see it

Only the people in your prayer group see your name, whether you finished today, your streak, your prayer requests and the group chat. A private message is seen only by you and the person you sent it to. Nobody outside your group can see any of it. There is no public feed.

Where it's stored

Your account and group are stored securely with our database provider (Supabase), and the app is built and hosted with Buildercult. Google sign-in is handled by Google. Bible chapters load from public Bible text services (bible-api.com and jsDelivr), which see a normal web request, like any website you visit.

Your walk

If you use the Walk, we keep the habits you choose, what you check off each day and your answers to "Where are you today?". Only you see these. If you turn on Hold me accountable, the people in your prayer group see one thing: how many of today's three flames are lit. Turn it off any time.

Payments

If you buy a membership, Apple, Google or our payment provider (Whop) handles the payment. We never see or store your card number. We keep which plan you have and when it renews, so it works on every device you sign in on.

Your country

To know whether everything is free where you live, the app asks Cloudflare which country your internet connection is in. We keep only the country, never your internet address.

Email updates

When you sign in, you agree that we may email you now and then with news from Live the Word. You can turn this off any time in You → Account, or reply to any email and ask. In the UK, Ireland and Canada, emails stay off unless you tick the box when you sign in.

What we never do

We never sell or rent your information, never share it with advertisers, never show ads in the app, and never share your prayer requests or messages with anyone outside your group. We only email you about Live the Word, and you can stop our emails any time.

Deleting your data

You can leave a group at any time, and remove your own prayer requests. To delete your account and everything in it, go to You → Account → Delete my account. It's permanent and happens right away.

Children

Live the Word is meant for people 13 and older. If you believe a child under 13 has made an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top. Questions or requests: livethewordapp@gmail.com.
